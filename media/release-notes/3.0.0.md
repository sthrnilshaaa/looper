## What's New

### New
- Player styles: Classic, Card, Arc and Linear, with accent, black or blurred-artwork backgrounds
- Swipe the artwork left or right to change songs
- Redesigned queue: pinned now-playing card, dice shuffle for upcoming songs, swipe to remove, and a Related tab from your own library
- Smart crossfade: beat-aware transitions between songs
- Headphone correction (AutoEQ) and per-output EQ profiles that switch when you change headphones
- Volume normalization with ReplayGain (track or album)
- Bit-perfect status and the Bluetooth codec in the audio quality details
- New lyrics sources: BetterLyrics, PaxSenix, BiniLyrics and Unison
- "Wrong lyrics" option to skip a lyrics source for a song
- Reorder Home sections: hold Home to enter reorder mode
- Account screen with your name and profile photo
- Options to keep the screen awake and to use the highest refresh rate
- Custom playlist cover photos and whole-app UI scale (Android)

### Improved
- New setup flow, settings and About screens
- Favorites and History open one page at a time, so big libraries load instantly
- Skeleton loaders on Home and Songs that match the real layout
- Smoother player: the progress wave runs at 30 fps and pauses in the background; lyrics redraw less often
- Fewer database writes during playback and a lighter home widget update
- Setup translated into all 14 app languages
- Bottom sheets blur consistently with your blur settings

### Fixed
- Playing a song no longer asks for Music & Audio access when All Files Access is already granted (GitHub build)
- Setup now offers Music & Audio access next to All Files Access
- Favorite state no longer flips back after a quick double toggle
- Rare crash from images with invalid sizes
